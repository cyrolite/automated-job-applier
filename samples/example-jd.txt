Graduate Hiring - Cybersecurity Engineer
BIT (formerly Matrixport) - Singapore

BIT is a global digital asset financial services and infrastructure group.

Job Responsibilities:
- Help build and improve the company's cybersecurity framework, including designing and implementing security protection systems.
- Analyze security threats and develop and refine security policies.
- Take part in the secure product development process, including security reviews, code analysis, and security testing.
- Keep up with industry trends, emerging threats, and defense mechanisms, and recommend improvements.
- Research blockchain and smart contract security, and propose technical solutions and new ideas.
- Prepare technical documentation and reports on cybersecurity activities.

Job Requirements:
- Fresh or recent graduates with under 2 years of full-time work experience.
- Degree in computer science, statistics, engineering, or a related field.
- Solid cybersecurity foundation, with familiarity with common threats and attack methods.
- Proficiency in at least one programming language (e.g., Python, Java, C/C++).
- Strong understanding of blockchain and smart contracts (a plus).
- Enthusiasm for blockchain, cryptocurrency, DeFi, and NFTs, and curiosity about new technologies.
- Dedicated, hardworking, and self-disciplined.
- Strong critical thinking and communication skills.
- Flexible, analytical thinker with strong problem-solving skills.
- Proactive self-starter who handles ambiguous and high-pressure situations well.
- Proficient in spoken and written English, with strong interpersonal and presentation skills (fluency in Mandarin a plus).
